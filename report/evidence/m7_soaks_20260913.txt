--- notes ---
D640 per-frame wall cost (~35-40 s) is dominated by untimed buffer I/O
(~3.1 MB per frame of os.pwrite/os.pread through the u-dma-buf driver copy
path: TX write + TX readback + 2.4 MB RX read), not by golden-model
computation: REF_POSITION_LIMIT=65536 (m7_switch.py) disables the software
reference for D640; per-frame validation is the frozen anchor SHA-256.
Timed datapath latency at D640: ~3.76-3.84 ms/frame.
